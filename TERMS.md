## 2. What LabRat is, and what it isn't

LabRat runs a small AI model on your phone and answers in English, in a short (Succinct) style,
from a local library that is built into the app.

- **Answers can be wrong.** A small model writes them, and they can be incomplete, outdated or simply
  mistaken, even with a source shown. Every answer the model writes ends with "May contain errors; check the source."
  Check anything important.
- **Not professional advice.** LabRat doesn't give medical, legal, financial or safety advice and
  doesn't replace a doctor, lawyer or other professional. Health content is AI-reviewed, not clinically
  reviewed.
- **Not for emergencies.** In an emergency, call your local emergency number. For health and dose
  questions the app may refuse to answer from memory and point you to emergency services instead.
- **Travel and visa information can be out of date.** Check official sources before you travel.

You are responsible for how you use LabRat's answers and for the decisions you make with them.
